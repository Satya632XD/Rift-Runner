SYNTHWAVE RIFT

Pilot a hovership through a procedurally generated neon canyon. Thread gates for boosts, skim the walls for style, and outrun the collapsing rift behind you. One crash ends the run.
Controls
Desktop: A/D or arrows. Mobile: drag left/right anywhere or tap the pads. Hold steady for boost chains.
Run rules
Gates boost score and rift buffer. Skimming walls adds style. Obstacle hit = game over.

SELECT LEVEL

Mercury through Neptune plus the endless run. Each planet uses a different palette, side lighting, and difficulty curve.

SETTINGS

Tune audio, steering, shake, and image intensity. Progress is stored locally.
